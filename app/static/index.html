The Tech Flow

Eight stages, one pipeline. Watch it run.

1

Intake

Decides whether you gave a topic or links.

2

Planner

Breaks a topic into focused search questions.

3

Searcher

Finds fresh sources on the live web.

4

Reader

Fetches web pages, PDFs, and YouTube transcripts. Reports any link it cannot read.

5

Organizer

Embeddings remove duplicates, rank relevance, and group sources into themes.

6

Summarizer

A transformer model condenses each source.

7

Writer

An LLM writes the brief using only those sources, with inline citations.

8

Critic

Checks every claim against the sources and sends weak drafts back for revision.

Built with LangGraph, Sentence Transformers, Hugging Face, Groq, and FastAPI.

Questions

Is it free?

Your first brief needs no account. After that, a free account unlocks more briefs and your saved history.

How long does a brief take?

Usually a few minutes. The pipeline reads and summarizes real sources, so it is thorough rather than instant.

Can it read PDFs and videos?

Yes. It reads web pages, PDFs, and YouTube videos that have transcripts. Anything it cannot read is reported back to you.

Can the brief be wrong?

Any AI system can make mistakes. That is why every claim is cited and a critic stage checks the draft, but you should verify anything important using the linked sources.
